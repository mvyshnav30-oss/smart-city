# 🌆 UrbanSync – Civicly

### Your City, Connected.

UrbanSync (Civicly) is a smart city information and services platform designed to make everyday urban life easier, smarter, and more connected. It provides citizens with a centralized place to explore city services, monitor city information, discover important locations, and access emergency resources.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://urbansync-omega.vercel.app/

## 🚀 Features

- **City Overview:** Get a quick overview of city conditions and updates.
- **City Services:** Explore essential public services in one place.
- **Transportation:** Access metro, rail, and city bus network information.
- **Healthcare:** Discover hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities.
- **Education:** Find schools, libraries, and learning resources.
- **Public Services:** Access permits, waste management, and other civic services.
- **Search & Filters:** Search and filter city services by category.
- **City Statistics:** View information about public transport, green spaces, and average commute times.
- **Important Locations:** Discover nearby civic offices, hospitals, and transit stations.
- **Emergency Information:** Access important safety resources and emergency contact information.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of UrbanSync is to improve the connection between citizens and their city by making essential information and services easier to discover and access.

It aims to support a smarter, more accessible, and more sustainable urban environment.
